  https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html . The pierre voice model itself is
  distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 by its authors; the project owner decided to ship it in public releases while accepting
  the risk that the base model's training data licence is held to reach it (decision D28, changed 2026-11-16). If that
  licence is challenged, the voice is withdrawn: its card is set back to "public": false and a new release is published
  without voices\pierre\.

grammar.sqlite — morphology table derived from Grammalecte « Lexique simplifié » v7.7 — MPL-2.0 (plan 11, from 10D-0)
    A separate data file built by src/Data.Build/build_grammar.py (GPL-3.0-or-later) from the same pinned French.lex
    (commit 08511c222029b3be20bf547563315d10fb48d44b, SHA-256 5938d8180b64e4f5320c358dab64e2eea1298c6c5967b147d5506f0ac4562c4a):
    every analysis of every form (form, lemma, tag) with the tags decoded into our own feature bits, and an index of the
    prediction lexicon's forms. Content: MPL-2.0 data derived from French.lex, indexed by the prediction lexicon's
    spellings (the index keys come from Lexique 3.83, CC BY-SA 4.0, see above: « coeur », « aujourd'hui »).
    grammar.sqlite is Covered Software under the MPL-2.0 (a Modification of French.lex, distributed in Executable Form,
    MPL-2.0 § 3.2); this GPL-3.0-or-later program is the Larger Work (MPL-2.0 § 3.3). Its Source Code Form is French.lex at
    the URL above plus the converter src/Data.Build/build_grammar.py. It is distributed with grammar/SOURCE.txt (source
    URL, commit, SHA-256 of the inputs and of the file, counts) and grammar/MPL-2.0.txt (the licence text, also in
    licenses/mpl-2.0/LICENSE.txt). Shipped next to Pepite.exe since test release 0.9.0 (plan 11, 10D-1: the agreement of
    the proposed words); credited in « À propos » as « Grammalecte — lexique (MPL-2.0) ».

    commit (GPL-3.0-or-later), lexicons/French.tagset.txt (documentation of the tags). See src/Data.Build/grammar.lock.json.
