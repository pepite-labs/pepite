No timeout; any other key, a jump, a hidden or rebuilt bar with another word there cancels a ring; it applies to the ≈ list too. A profile, « Classe » or user file that never set them gets `listenDouble` and true (the first version's `words.listenFirst` was never released: no migration), an explicit value is kept, a machine lock wins (no schema bump). Two settings, **off by default**: A « Maj + F1…F8 : écouter le mot » (`words.listenShiftKeys`, needs the F-keys) and D « Bouton haut-parleur sous chaque mot » (`words.listenButton`): a full-width speaker band **under the word**, inside the tile — the bar gets taller, never wider: band S 24 · M 26 · L 30 · XL 36 DIP — and **with D on a click / tap on the word inserts at once whatever the click mode** (the band is the listen gesture of the mouse and touch; the « Clic sur un mot » list is greyed with a note). Right-click, Maj+F*n* and the band listen (and arm the tile when a confirming press exists), except that the right-click inserts in `listenDouble`. F1–F8 remain the only insert keys. |
